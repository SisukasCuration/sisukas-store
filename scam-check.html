Read This First

This is not purchase, financial, or investment advice. Sisukas is not recommending that you buy, sell, or pay any particular amount for anything. What you do with this number is entirely your decision.

These are general averages, not valuations. Each figure is a rough average of secondary-market (resale) prices across a wide mix of styles, releases, sizes, and colorways within a category — it is not an appraisal of any individual item.

Resale only. The numbers reflect approximate secondary-market resale prices, not original retail, not what a store would charge, and not a guaranteed sale or buy price.

Real value varies a lot. A specific piece's worth depends on the exact style, hype, size, flaws, completeness (tags, box, receipt), and current demand — none of which this tool measures.

Authenticity is not checked here. This tool says nothing about whether an item is real. For that, see the Authentication Ledger.

Estimates can be out of date. Markets move. Always do your own research before making any decision.
